# Messaging

You can chat with Poppet through a Discord or Slack bot that you create. The bot only answers you, and it replies in threads. It can also post the results of [scheduled tasks](../tasks.md) to a channel you choose.

- [Discord](discord.md): create a bot, connect it, and chat in threads.
- [Slack](slack.md): create a Slack app, connect it, and chat in threads.

Messaging needs the [worker](../worker.md) running. Terminal chat works without it.

Discord and Slack are also the only places you can [approve commands](../commands.md#approve-a-command) that need your permission.
